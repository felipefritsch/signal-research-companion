# Signal research companion

**Start with [Lesson 1](docs/modules/01-research-loop.md).** This is a private, offline-first learning project. The full route takes an estimated 34 hours, plus up to six optional hours. You have not been assessed; generated examples are not evidence of your mastery.

The foundation is ready: a full roadmap, seven module guides, executable worked-example notebooks, an offline demonstrator, and an OpenRouter adapter tested with simulated responses. Lessons 2–7 are deliberately concise launch notes to expand as we reach them. This is not a completed 40-hour course or a live-agent benchmark.

## Choose today's mode

- **Normal energy:** read the intuition, run a small example, change one important part, discuss the result.
- **Low energy:** read the worked example and its diagram. Skip the checkpoint. Resume from the same place next time.
- **Want the finished version:** ask for a walkthrough of the implementation; no assessment is implied.

For the first sitting, allow 25–35 minutes: Lesson 1 through the numerical example, then notebook 01. The rest of Stage 1 can happen later. Keep at most three active priorities: the research loop, decision quality, and evidence/evaluation boundaries.
