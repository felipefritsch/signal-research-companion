## Revision and worked example

The public corpus contains two quarterly releases for each of six issuers at the fixed cutoff. Fiscal labels differ: Nvidia FY2027 quarters are not errors merely because releases occurred in calendar 2026. Preserve both concepts.

The inventory is frozen before acquisition/processing. Each acquisition records its URL, retrieval time, content hash and status. Current copies may have changed since release; an official publication date is not proof of an intraday historical information set.

The public notebook checks the frozen inventory, coverage and short evidence records. It does not invoke an LLM or infer returns. Its role is to transfer evidence bookkeeping from synthetic examples to actual documents.

Build the dossier as hypothesis → evidence → specification → result → critique → decision. A proposed hypothesis about investment spending or guidance remains a proposal until the target, information set, comparator and test are defined.
