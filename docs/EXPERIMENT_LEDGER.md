## Evidence consumption

The synthetic development and transfer fixtures are visible and have been inspected in building this demonstrator. They are **consumed diagnostic examples**, not a pristine holdout. New learner challenges can be fresh to the learner without becoming a scientific held-out benchmark. Future agent evaluation requires separate, inaccessible acceptance evidence.

Record each future trial's hypothesis, purpose, allowed edits, data/model/prompt versions, search history, resource use, failures, result and decision. Never delete negative variants. A changed research question starts a new specification.
