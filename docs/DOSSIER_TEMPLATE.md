# Research dossier — fill before and after the experiment

This is a reusable template. Unfilled fields are unresolved, not passed checks. Label the work synthetic, public descriptive evidence, retrospective research or prospective evaluation.

## Before observing results

- **Question and hypothesis:** mechanism, expected sign, population, horizon and falsifiers. What non-return implication could distinguish the story?
- **Data and information:** source IDs/hashes, versions, universe/eligibility, release/receipt times, model vintage, missingness and revisions. What could the agent or researcher know?
- **Target and execution:** forecast quantity, label window, entry/exit rule, overlapping outcomes, turnover/cost assumptions, borrow/liquidity constraints where relevant.
- **Comparison:** simple baseline, incumbent signals, temporal splits, permitted edits, preprocessing fit windows, candidate budget and prior search history.
- **Evaluation protection:** who can see labels/results; what remains outside agent access; independent arithmetic/semantic validators; when final evidence becomes consumed.
- **Resource limits:** total calls, wall-clock budget, provider/model settings, approved spending and stopping rules. Record the freeze/version before running.

## After the run

- **Evidence:** source-supported observations, exact numerical results, failures and unresolved records. Link machine-readable outputs and traces.
- **Prediction:** comparison with baseline, uncertainty appropriate to dependence and sample size, robustness and placebo tests. State which tests remain unrun.
- **Implementation:** exposure/concentration, costs, timing, liquidity/capacity limits and whether the return proxy supports the claim.
- **Additivity:** candidate-on-incumbent and reverse comparisons; marginal forecast/portfolio value under the same frozen design. Correlation alone is insufficient.
- **Critique:** alternative explanations, leakage channels, search selection, regressions and resource cost. Separate observed defects from speculative concerns.
- **Decision:** promote / revise / park / kill, the concrete reason and what evidence would change it. Promotion advances research; it does not authorize trading.
- **Monitoring and retirement:** if later promoted, specify observable failures that trigger review or retirement. No monitoring service is created by filling this template.

For a harness-only experiment, replace financial outcomes with task validity, evidence support, failure rate and quality/cost measures. Do not turn task-quality gains into financial claims.
