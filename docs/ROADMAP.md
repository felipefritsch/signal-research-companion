## Pacing

Use milestones rather than a calendar. A 25–35 minute sitting is enough to move one concept forward; longer implementation sessions are optional. The first three active priorities are the research loop, decision quality and evidence/evaluation boundaries. Do not add the entire roadmap to a daily queue.

## Optional model-efficiency branch

The [OpenRouter module](OPENROUTER.md) connects Stages 2, 3, 4 and 6: compare models for routing, extraction, critique and synthesis; include diffusion models where listed and supported. Start with its offline cost calculations. Live runs require a selected task set, model/provider choices and a separately approved budget. This is part of the optional six-hour allowance, not an extra mandatory course.

## Final project

Deliver the reusable lab, research dossier, visual report and interview walkthrough. The final empirical work remains conditional on data validity and live-run access. A small synthetic counterexample demonstrates a mechanism; it does not establish how an actual model behaves. Negative findings are retained.
